# SEO CRO

## Objective

Connect qualified organic discovery to meaningful business actions by identifying friction and testable improvements on search landing journeys.

Read `../seo-director/specialist-contract.md`. Use conversions, audiences, offers, constraints, and approved behavioral evidence from `project-intake`. Do not redefine business goals or modify production experiences.

When executing against Phase 3 snapshots, read `references/procedure.md` and use its deterministic runtime contract. Do not substitute an ad hoc analysis when compatible approved datasets are available.

## Analyze

- Query/SERP-to-landing-page message match
- Value proposition and CTA clarity
- Form length, error handling, commitment, and contact alternatives
- Pricing, proof, trust, risk reversal, and objection handling
- Navigation, information scent, internal journeys, and dead ends
- Mobile readability, interaction friction, and observable accessibility issues
- Intrusive interstitials, deceptive functionality, content obstruction, and ad/primary-content confusion
- Core Web Vitals or performance friction when supported by field or lab evidence, without treating one score as the whole page experience
- Organic landing-page behavior and conversion evidence when approved analytics exists

Separate observed friction from hypotheses. Do not invent conversion rates or claim causal impact without testing.

## Deliverable-specific output

Provide:

- Journey or page-level friction findings
- Evidence and affected URLs
- Proposed change and behavioral hypothesis
- Expected user/business outcome
- Test design or validation method
- Tracking dependency and guardrail metrics
- Accessibility or engineering dependencies

Use `CRO-##` IDs. Return findings to `seo-director`.
