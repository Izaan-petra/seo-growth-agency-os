# SEO Opportunity Scoring Framework

## Scale

Score each factor from 1 to 5.

- Business value: contribution to leads, sales, revenue or strategic visibility
- SEO impact: effect on crawlability, relevance, authority, visibility or conversions
- Traffic potential: relative qualified-traffic opportunity
- Conversion potential: alignment with meaningful actions
- Strategic fit: alignment with business model and priorities
- Confidence: quality of evidence
- Urgency: how quickly it should be addressed
- Effort: implementation cost; higher means harder
- Competition: ranking difficulty; higher means harder
- Time to impact: 1 immediate, 5 long-term/uncertain

## Formula

`Opportunity Score = (Business Value + SEO Impact + Traffic Potential + Conversion Potential + Strategic Fit + Confidence + Urgency) - (Effort + Competition)`

This is comparative, not predictive.

## Priority bands

- 26–33: Critical
- 21–25: High
- 15–20: Medium
- 9–14: Low
- 8 or below: Defer or reject

Manual overrides are allowed for severe blockers such as accidental noindex, crawl blocking, broken revenue pages, migration failures or tracking failures. Explain the override.

## Classifications

- Critical fix
- Quick win
- Growth opportunity
- Strategic project
- Maintenance
- Validation required
- Defer
- Reject

## Quick-win criteria

A quick win requires:

- Business value at least 3
- SEO impact at least 3
- Confidence at least 4
- Effort no more than 2
- Competition no more than 3
- No major unresolved dependency

## Review rules

Do not overestimate impact, underestimate effort, ignore competition, or prioritize informational traffic solely because it may be large. Long-term authority work should not be unfairly penalized only because it takes time.
