# Quality Examples

## Weak vs strong recommendation

### Weak
Improve the service page content.

### Strong
The `/services/roof-repair/` page explains the service but does not address common decision-stage questions, project evidence, service-area coverage or emergency-response expectations. Add sections covering process, response times, repair-versus-replacement guidance, recent project examples, FAQs and a stronger request-a-quote CTA. Link to this page from relevant location pages and informational articles. Validate impact through Search Console query growth and organic lead submissions.

## Weak vs strong technical finding

### Weak
Fix canonical tags.

### Strong
Several important category pages appear to reference a different URL variant in their canonical tags. Verify the canonical destination on each template and ensure it points directly to the preferred, indexable HTTPS URL without redirects. This should be treated as high priority because incorrect canonicals can consolidate signals away from revenue-generating pages. Validate with a fresh crawl and Search Console URL inspection.

## Weak vs strong new-page recommendation

### Weak
Create more blog posts about CRM software.

### Strong
Create `/crm-for-real-estate/` as a commercial use-case page for real-estate teams evaluating CRM solutions. The page should cover lead capture, follow-up automation, pipeline stages, integrations, reporting, role-based workflows, migration concerns, a comparison table and a product demonstration CTA. It should link to relevant feature pages and receive internal links from the CRM guide, integrations hub and industry navigation.

## Weak vs strong GEO recommendation

### Weak
Add FAQ schema for AI visibility.

### Strong
The site does not clearly explain who the company serves, where it operates, or how its service differs from alternatives. Strengthen the Organization and service entities through consistent company details, expert profiles, concise service definitions, cited first-party evidence and comparison content. Add structured data only where it accurately reflects visible content. Schema alone should not be presented as an AI-visibility solution.

## Strong executive-summary style

The website has a credible service offering and several indexable commercial pages, but organic growth is constrained by weak page specialization, limited supporting content and unclear internal-link pathways. The strongest near-term opportunity is to improve the highest-value service pages and connect them to a focused set of informational and location-based support pages. Technical risks should be validated first, particularly canonical consistency and sitemap quality. Because first-party analytics were unavailable, traffic potential is directional and should be refined after Search Console and conversion data are reviewed.

## Confidence labeling example

- Verified: The homepage links to four primary service pages.
- High-confidence inference: Lead generation appears to be the main conversion model based on repeated quote-request CTAs.
- Medium-confidence inference: The business likely prioritizes the UK market based on address, currency and service-area language.
- Requires first-party validation: Which service has the highest revenue and lead-close rate.
