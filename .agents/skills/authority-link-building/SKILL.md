# Authority and Link Building

## Objective

Identify credible ways to earn relevant authority, qualified referral exposure, and editorial links without manipulative tactics.

Read `../seo-director/specialist-contract.md`. Use only backlink, competitor, market, and target-page evidence approved by `project-intake`. Do not run a separate Ahrefs intake or contact prospects without explicit authorization.

## Analyze

- Baseline referring-domain, backlink, anchor, target-page, and new/lost patterns
- Business and organic competitor link gaps
- Reclamation, broken-link, unlinked-mention, and partner opportunities
- Linkable assets, original research, tools, statistics, expert commentary, and case studies
- Digital PR themes, resource pages, associations, directories, interviews, and communities
- Prospect relevance, editorial quality, traffic plausibility, placement context, and outbound-link behavior
- Outbound-link qualification for compensated, affiliate, and user-generated links

Reject links bought or sold for ranking credit, PBNs, excessive exchanges, automated link creation, irrelevant or low-quality directories, forum-comment spam, fake reviews, deceptive outreach, optimized anchors distributed through paid articles/widgets/templates, and guaranteed links. Compensated and affiliate links must use `rel="sponsored"` or, where appropriate, `nofollow`; mark user-generated links with `rel="ugc"` when applicable. Never treat a link attribute as a guaranteed crawl block or qualify a prospect on third-party authority metrics alone.

## Deliverable-specific output

Provide:

- Backlink baseline and limitations
- Competitor/link-gap findings
- Target-page and linkable-asset mapping
- Prospect categories and qualification criteria
- Reclamation and digital PR opportunities
- Anchor/context guardrails
- Paid, affiliate, UGC, and outbound-link qualification requirements
- Outreach angles for later human-approved execution
- Tracking fields and validation metrics

Use `AUTH-##` IDs. Return findings to `seo-director`.
