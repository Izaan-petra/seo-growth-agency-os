# SEO Content Strategy

## Objective

Improve how existing and proposed pages satisfy search intent, demonstrate expertise, support conversion journeys, and connect into a coherent topic architecture.

Read `../seo-director/specialist-contract.md`. Consume the intake record plus approved competitor, SERP, keyword, and technical inputs. Do not repeat business discovery or set the final publishing roadmap.

When executing against Phase 3 snapshots, read `references/procedure.md` and use its deterministic runtime contract. Do not substitute an ad hoc analysis when compatible approved datasets are available.

## Analyze

- People-first intent alignment, originality, completeness, first-hand expertise where relevant, site-purpose fit, freshness, and evidence quality
- Thin, duplicate, outdated, or cannibalizing pages
- Missing commercial, support, comparison, use-case, location, proof, and case-study content
- Titles, headings, entity/topic coverage, media, CTAs, and internal links
- Refresh, consolidate, redirect, retain, or remove options
- New pages justified by business relevance and observed intent
- Authorship, sourcing, review, methodology, and trust needs proportionate to the topic's risk
- Scaled-content, scraped-content, doorway-page, keyword-stuffing, fake-freshness, expired-domain, and site-reputation-abuse risks when applicable

Avoid creating pages solely because a keyword exists, prescribing a Google word-count target, or changing dates without substantive updates. Require unique value, operational truth, credible evidence, and a clear role in the customer journey. Treat generative AI as a production method, not a quality exemption: reject scaled output created primarily to manipulate rankings regardless of whether people or automation produced it.

## Deliverable-specific output

Provide:

- Existing-content action table by URL
- Content gaps with supporting evidence
- New-page opportunities
- Full briefs for material pages: proposed URL/title, audience, intent, topics/entities, required sections, questions, unique evidence or first-hand contribution, author/reviewer needs, internal links, CTA, schema opportunity, dependencies, and validation
- Editorial and subject-matter-review requirements

Use `CONTENT-##` IDs. Return findings and briefs to `seo-director`.
