# Keyword and Intent Strategy

## Objective

Translate approved search evidence and business context into coherent intent clusters and page targets that can attract qualified demand.

Read `../seo-director/specialist-contract.md`. Use the audiences, markets, offerings, conversions, and evidence inventory from `project-intake`. Do not request new platform access or finalize the execution roadmap.

When executing against Phase 3 snapshots, read `references/procedure.md` and use its deterministic runtime contract. Do not substitute an ad hoc analysis when compatible approved datasets are available.

## Analyze

- Transactional, commercial, informational, comparison, local, branded, and conversational intent
- Problem-aware, solution-aware, and product-aware stages
- Existing query/page performance when approved GSC or rank data is available
- Third-party keyword estimates when intake approves them
- SERP intent and page-type fit
- Existing-page targeting, cannibalization, consolidation, and missing-page needs
- Topic/entity relationships and internal-link implications
- Site-purpose fit and the unique value required before recommending a new target page

Never fabricate volume, difficulty, CPC, rankings, or traffic. When quantitative evidence is unavailable, use clearly labeled relative demand, competition, conversion potential, and confidence. Do not recommend keyword stuffing, doorway pages, scaled near-duplicate pages, or a fixed word count as a Google requirement.

## Deliverable-specific output

Provide a cluster map with:

- Cluster and representative queries/topics
- Intent and audience stage
- Market/language
- Existing or proposed target URL
- Page type
- Business relevance and conversion role
- Evidence-backed demand/competition assessment
- Differentiation requirement
- Cannibalization or dependency notes

Use `KEYWORD-##` IDs. Return the map and findings to `seo-director`.
