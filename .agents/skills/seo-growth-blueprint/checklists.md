# SEO Growth Blueprint Checklists

Use these statuses: Reviewed—no meaningful issue, Issue found, Opportunity found, Not applicable, Could not verify, Requires first-party validation.

## Access and discovery

- Completed intake and director brief supplied
- Selected specialist outputs supplied or explicitly marked missing
- URL and preferred-host facts carried from intake or specialist evidence
- HTTPS and redirect behavior checked
- Relevant page-access evidence supplied by selected specialists
- Business model, audience, offerings, markets and conversions carried from intake
- Assumptions and limitations documented

## Technical SEO

- Google's minimum technical eligibility checked without implying an indexing guarantee
- Status codes, redirects and broken links
- robots.txt and XML sitemap
- Meta robots and X-Robots-Tag where observable, with crawl access required for `noindex`
- Canonicals and signal consistency; crawl blocking/removals not used as canonical substitutes
- Soft 404 and removed-URL handling
- URL consistency and duplication
- Architecture, crawl depth and orphan risks
- Internal linking and breadcrumbs
- Pagination, facets and parameters
- Mobile usability
- Performance and Core Web Vitals risks
- JavaScript rendering risks
- Mobile-first content, metadata, image, directive and structured-data parity
- Lazy-loaded or infinite-scroll content discoverability
- HTTPS and mixed content
- Hreflang and international targeting
- Structured data relevance, visible-content match and current feature eligibility
- Image/video discovery and snippet-preview controls where applicable
- Crawl-budget work limited to sites that justify it

## On-page SEO

- Titles and meta descriptions
- H1 and heading hierarchy
- Search-intent satisfaction
- People-first originality, completeness, first-hand contribution, site-purpose fit and substantive freshness
- Entity and topic coverage
- Calls to action
- Images, alt text and media

## Content strategy

- Thin, duplicate or outdated content
- Cannibalization risks
- Missing commercial pages
- Missing support, comparison, use-case, location and case-study pages
- Refresh, consolidate, redirect or remove opportunities
- New pages justified by business value and search intent
- Keyword stuffing, doorway pages, scaled-content abuse, scraping, fake freshness, expired-domain abuse and site-reputation abuse rejected

## Competitors and SERPs

- Business competitors separated from organic competitors
- Ranking page types identified
- SERP features and intent reviewed
- Competitor strengths, weaknesses and gaps assessed
- Website’s realistic ability to compete considered

## GEO, AEO and trust

- Entity clarity and organization information
- Author/reviewer identity and credentials
- Original evidence, research and citations
- Direct answers, definitions and question headings
- Structured data supports visible content
- No special Google AI schema or optimization requirement claimed
- Intended Google AI-supporting pages are indexable and snippet eligible
- About, contact, ownership and editorial transparency
- E-E-A-T, case studies and testimonials
- External mentions and citation-worthiness

## Authority

- Linkable assets
- Digital PR and partnerships
- Relevant directories and local citations
- Unlinked mentions
- Expert commentary, interviews and podcasts
- Spam and manipulative tactics rejected
- Paid, affiliate and UGC outbound links appropriately qualified

## CRO and UX

- Message match
- CTA clarity
- Forms and friction
- Trust and proof
- Navigation and mobile experience
- Pricing and objection handling
- Conversion tracking marked for validation

## Measurement

- Search Console
- GA4
- Bing Webmaster Tools
- Conversion and lead tracking
- Rank, backlink, crawl and Core Web Vitals monitoring
- Branded/non-branded reporting with Search Console privacy and row-limit caveats
- Search Console clicks/canonical assignment kept distinct from GA4 sessions/landing URLs
- GA4 attribution, modeling, thresholding, sampling and freshness documented
- Generative-AI reporting requested only when available; overall-total inclusion and dedicated-report rollout documented

## Final QA

- Included and excluded workstreams match the director brief
- Material findings retain specialist IDs and source provenance
- Missing workstreams and conflicting findings are surfaced for director review
- Where relevant, specialist inspection evidence and exact URLs are preserved
- Important claims supported by evidence
- Facts and assumptions separated
- No invented metrics or guarantees
- Google-specific claims distinguish requirements, policies, eligibility, recommendations, reporting limits and hypotheses
- Change-sensitive Google guidance was rechecked and dated
- Search intent, SERP format, competition and business value considered
- Recommendations include evidence, dependencies and validation needs for director review
- Director-supplied priorities, owners and roadmap are clearly distinguished from specialist output
- Selected workstreams have proportionate coverage; unselected workstreams are explicitly excluded
