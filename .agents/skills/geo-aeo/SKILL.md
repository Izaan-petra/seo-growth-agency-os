# GEO and AEO

## Objective

Improve the site's observable readiness to be accurately understood, summarized, trusted, and cited by search and answer systems.

Read `../seo-director/specialist-contract.md`. Use organization, audience, market, and evidence facts from `project-intake`. Do not promise AI citations or treat schema alone as a visibility strategy.

When executing against Phase 3 snapshots, read `references/procedure.md` and use its deterministic runtime contract. Do not substitute an ad hoc analysis when compatible approved datasets are available.

## Analyze

- Organization, product/service, person, location, and topic entity clarity
- Consistency of names, ownership, contact details, credentials, and external profiles
- Author/reviewer identity and subject-matter qualifications
- Direct answers, definitions, question structures, comparisons, and explanatory completeness
- Original research, first-party evidence, citations, methodology, and freshness
- Structured data alignment with visible content
- About, editorial, corrections, sourcing, and trust transparency
- External corroboration and citation-worthiness
- Indexability and snippet eligibility for pages intended to support Google AI features
- Snippet-preview controls and their visibility tradeoffs when the publisher needs content restrictions
- Merchant Center and Business Profile consistency when ecommerce or local visibility is in scope

For Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, apply standard Search fundamentals: Google documents no special schema, AI text file, or optimization requirement. Use Googlebot and Search preview controls for Search participation or excerpts; do not misrepresent Google-Extended as the Search AI-feature control. Distinguish observable readiness from actual inclusion, never invent mentions or model behavior, and never guarantee citation or visibility.

## Deliverable-specific output

Provide:

- Entity and trust gap assessment
- Answer-ready content opportunities
- Citation-worthiness improvements
- Structured-data recommendations tied to visible content
- Author/editorial transparency actions
- External corroboration needs and validation approach
- Google AI-feature eligibility or preview-control constraints, when applicable

Use `GEO-##` IDs. Return findings to `seo-director`.
